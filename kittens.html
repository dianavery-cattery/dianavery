Our Maternity Nursery

When the Queen's time to give birth approaches, we provide her with a quiet and safe place, away from the hustle and bustle of the household. This is usually our bedroom, where the environment allows the future mum to feel safe and calm while she gives birth and cares for her newborn kittens. The isolated environment helps protect the fragile kittens during their most critical first weeks, reduces stress for the mum and allows for proper monitoring of feeding, warmth, and bonding. From the moment the kittens are born, we are around them and mom 24/7 to make sure everyone is okay. Once the kittens are stronger and more mobile, the family moves to a larger space, and when they are completely stable we introduce them to our other cats and then they have complete freedom in our home.
At 4-5 weeks of age we start weaning our kittens on a balanced diet and toilet training them. Most kittens are fully toilet trained by 6-8 weeks of age. We share photos and videos of the kittens' progress on our social media so that new families can feel connected to them before our little furry creatures move into their new wonderful homes. Kittens cannot be officially reserved until they are 6-8 weeks old and we have not made a decision on who will stay with us.

Each of our kittens:
* Is ready for re-home after 13 weeks old
* Has vet check-ups before leave us
* Received deworming and vaccinations age appropriate
* Has microchip

Kittens for Sale

Please note that we strictly adhere to all ethical breeding rules and strongly dissociate ourselves from backyard breeding. We do not breed for profit. Our Queens give birth once a year to allow them to recover and enjoy the rest of their time. We place our kittens directly into private homes. Our kittens are never sold to pet stores, brokers, farms, shelters or laboratories. If an owner can no longer care for a kitten, we ask that they contact us first so that we can help secure responsible placement.

We provide:
* Certificate
* Pedigree
* Five weeks free insurance
* Vet card & Medical history
* Kitten goodie bag including food & toys.
* Information leaflets and small brochures, as well as lifelong support with advice on the care and well-being of our kittens from their new parents.

Please make sure there are no members with cat allergies in your home before contacting us.
To reserve a kitten we require a non-refundable deposited of £300. We refund this deposit if we withdraw the kitten from sale.

We will be happy to advise you and answer all your questions.
